OUR STORY

A legacy of
Gujarati goodness.

A familiar taste, a timeless tradition.

Shree Jay Jalaram Khaman House celebrates the comforting flavours of Gujarati farsan. With a tradition dating back to 1990, our name stands for the joy of sharing freshly prepared snacks with family, friends and neighbours.

From soft khaman and delicate khandvi to crisp samosas and savoury patra, every visit is an invitation to enjoy the taste of tradition.
